Ready to power down?

Orbit shutdown sequence

Before shutting down, make sure your work is safe. Windows does not expose a reliable way for Orbit to list every unsaved item across all apps.

⚠ Please check these before continuing
  • Save open documents, projects, and browser forms.
  • Finish downloads, exports, uploads, and file copies.
  • Close apps that may be installing or updating.
  • Orbit cannot inspect every unsaved file; this is a reminder, not a detected list.

Orbit will play its own short animation, then request Windows to shut down. The normal Windows shutdown screen may still appear afterward.
