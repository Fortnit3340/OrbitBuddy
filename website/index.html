Shutdown, with a moment to think.

Double-click your buddy to open a clear shutdown warning. Orbit plays a custom animation before requesting Windows to shut down.

  • Reminder to save documents and finish downloads
  • Cancel or continue — you choose
  • Cancel the animation before shutdown begins
✓Save your work and browser forms
✓Finish downloads and file transfers
✓Confirm before the shutdown sequence
🌌Custom Orbit animation

Note: Windows does not let ordinary apps reliably inspect unsaved changes in every program or replace the native shutdown screen. Orbit warns you, animates, then requests shutdown.
